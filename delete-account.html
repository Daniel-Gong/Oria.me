Delete your Oria AI account

TheOther Intelligence LLC · Last updated September 2026

This page is for the Oria AI app (iOS and Android, developer TheOther Intelligence LLC). Use it to request or complete deletion of your account and the personal data we store with it. Deletion is permanent.

How to delete your account

  1. On this page. Sign in with the same email, Google, or Apple account you use for Oria AI, then confirm deletion below.
  2. In the Oria AI app. Open Account → About → Delete Account, then confirm. This is available on both iOS and Android.
  3. By email. If you cannot sign in, write to privacy@theother.me from the address on the account and ask us to delete it. We complete email requests within 30 days.

Cancel any Apple or Google Play subscription separately in that store. Deleting your Oria AI account does not automatically stop store billing.

Delete on this page

Sign in, then confirm. This uses the same Oria AI account as the iOS and Android apps.

Self-serve deletion is temporarily unavailable. Email privacy@theother.me and we will delete the account.

Data that is deleted

When you delete your Oria AI account, we delete:

  • Your sign-in account (email and authentication credentials)
  • Profile information (name, username, addresses, and other profile fields you entered)
  • Conversations with Oria, notes, mood check-ins, insights, memories, and coaching plans
  • Location history, place labels, and other device-synced activity stored in Oria
  • Stored Health Data summaries (daily metrics, insight inputs, coaching observations, food-photo nutrition, and friend-challenge entries). Raw records in Health Connect or Apple Health stay on your device; revoke those permissions separately if you want the app to stop reading them
  • Social posts you created in Moments, plus your likes and comments
  • App preferences, notification settings, and connected-service tokens stored by Oria

Data that may be kept, and for how long

  • Store billing records. Apple and Google keep their own purchase and subscription records. Cancel in the App Store or Google Play if you have an active plan. We may retain limited billing identifiers as required for tax, accounting, or fraud prevention, typically up to the period required by law (often up to 7 years for tax records).
  • Legal and security records. We may keep a minimal record of the deletion request (for example, that an account was deleted and when) as needed to handle disputes or abuse.
  • Backups. Copies in routine backups are purged within 30 days.
  • Third-party AI providers. Data already sent to providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google to generate a response cannot be recalled from those providers. We do not keep sending them data after your account is deleted. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Waitlist or newsletter. These are separate from the app account. Email privacy@theother.me if you also want those addresses removed.

Questions

Privacy requests: privacy@theother.me. Company: TheOther Intelligence LLC. See also Privacy and Contact.
